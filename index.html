How to play

Every day is a three-word gauntlet on one gallows: an Around Town word, one From the Brief, and a short, nasty Stinger to finish. Guess one letter at a time — tap the keyboard or type.

You get six lives for the whole run. Every wrong letter, on any word, draws one more piece of the little chalk guy — six misses anywhere and he hangs. The keyboard resets for each new word; the gallows doesn't.

The Stinger's hint stays locked until your third miss — stay clean and you're on your own.

The clock starts on your first guess and runs across all three words. Winning runs make the monthly leaderboard: lives kept matter most, speed breaks ties — a clean no-miss gauntlet tops the board.

Three fresh words every midnight (Vermont time, obviously).

A production of the BTown Brief, Burlington's favorite newsletter.
